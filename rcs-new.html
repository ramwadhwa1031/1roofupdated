Everything you need to know about RCS Business Messaging

Rich Communication Services (RCS) is the next generation of SMS that allows businesses to send rich media, interactive messages, and provide a more engaging experience than traditional text messages.

Unlike SMS, RCS supports rich media (images, videos, buttons), read receipts, typing indicators, and interactive elements, providing a more engaging and app-like experience within the native messaging app.

RCS is supported on most Android devices through Google Messages and is being adopted by carriers worldwide. It's automatically available on most new Android smartphones.

Pricing varies based on volume and features. Contact us for a customized quote based on your business needs and expected message volume.

Getting started is easy! Simply contact our team, and we'll guide you through the setup process, including business verification, message template approval, and system integration.
